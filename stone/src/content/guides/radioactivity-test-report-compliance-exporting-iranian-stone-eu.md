# Radioactivity Test Report and Compliance for Exporting Iranian Stone to the EU

When exporting natural stone to the European Union, radioactivity compliance is a regulatory requirement that cannot be overlooked. Many EU member states require a radioactivity test report confirming that construction materials — including natural stone — meet defined thresholds for naturally occurring radioactive materials (NORM). Without this report, your shipment may be held at EU customs or rejected entirely.
