# Iranian Natural Stone Export Documentation: Bill of Lading and Packing List

The bill of lading and packing list are the two most critical shipping documents in any Iranian natural stone export. Together, they tell the shipping line, customs authorities, and you exactly what is in the container, where it is going, and who owns it. Errors in these documents cause delays, extra costs, and in worst cases, cargo being held at the destination port.
