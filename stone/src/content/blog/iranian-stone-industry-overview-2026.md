# Iranian Stone Industry Overview 2026: Quarries, Capacity and Export Outlook

Iran's natural stone industry is one of the largest in the world. With vast geological reserves spanning travertine, marble, granite, onyx, limestone, and crystal stone, the country is a major producer and exporter of dimension stone. As we look at 2026, the industry stands at a pivotal point — with growing demand from Asia, the Middle East, and Europe, and increasing investment in quarry modernization and export infrastructure.

## Quarry Landscape and Production Capacity

Iran has thousands of active quarries across its provinces. The major stone-producing regions include West Azerbaijan (Urmia), East Azerbaijan, Isfahan (Natanz), Fars (Dehbid, Neyriz), Markazi (Mahallat), and Qazvin. Travertine from Azarshahr and Takab remains the flagship export product, followed by Pietra Gray marble from Lar and Dehbid, and granite from Natanz.

The country's annual dimension stone production capacity exceeds 25 million tons, though actual output is influenced by domestic demand, export market conditions, and quarry operational factors. Iran consistently ranks among the top five stone-producing nations globally.

## Export Markets and Trends

Iranian stone exports serve a diverse range of markets. China is the largest single destination, importing substantial volumes of marble and travertine blocks for processing. The Middle East — particularly Iraq, Turkey, and the Gulf states — imports finished slabs and blocks for construction projects. European buyers increasingly source Iranian marble and travertine for architectural and restoration projects.

## The Bandar Abbas Export Corridor

Nearly all containerized stone exports flow through Bandar Abbas, Iran's largest port on the Persian Gulf. The port's connectivity to major shipping routes makes it the natural export gateway for stone bound for Asia, Europe, and beyond.

## Outlook for 2026

The 2026 outlook is positive. Quarry modernization, improved quality control, and streamlined export documentation are making Iranian stone more accessible to international buyers. For project contractors and stone importers, the combination of competitive FOB pricing, diverse stone varieties, and reliable shipping from Bandar Abbas positions Iran as a key supply source.
