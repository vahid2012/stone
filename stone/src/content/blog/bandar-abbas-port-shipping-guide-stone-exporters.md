# Bandar Abbas Port: A Shipping Guide for Iranian Stone Exporters

Bandar Abbas is Iran's largest and most active port, handling the vast majority of the country's containerized stone exports. Located on the Persian Gulf, it offers direct access to major shipping routes connecting Asia, the Middle East, Europe, and beyond. For stone exporters and importers alike, understanding how Bandar Abbas works is essential for planning efficient, cost-effective shipments.

## Port Overview

Bandar Abbas (Shahid Rajaee Port) is a modern container port with multiple terminals, gantry cranes, and extensive container yards. It serves all major shipping lines operating in the Persian Gulf, including MSC, Maersk, and regional carriers. The port handles both 20-foot and 40-foot containers, with specialized areas for heavy cargo like stone blocks.

## Shipping Lines and Routes

Major shipping lines serve Bandar Abbas with direct and transshipment services to ports worldwide. Key routes include:

- **Asia**: Direct services to Xiamen, Shuitou, Qingdao (China); Mundra, Nhava Sheva (India)
- **Middle East**: Direct to Jebel Ali (UAE), Dammam (Saudi Arabia), Doha (Qatar)
- **Europe**: Transshipment services to European ports via hub ports like Jebel Ali or Singapore

## Container Handling for Stone

Stone blocks are heavy — a 20-foot container typically holds 25–28 tons. Proper bracing, dunnage, and weight distribution are critical to prevent container damage and ensure safe loading. The port has weighbridges to verify container weight before loading.

## Tips for Smooth Exports

1. Book vessel space 2–3 weeks in advance, especially during peak season
2. Ensure all documents are ready before the container arrives at port
3. Verify container weight does not exceed destination road limits
4. Work with an experienced customs broker at Bandar Abbas
