# Travertine vs Marble vs Granite: Which Iranian Natural Stone Is Right for Your Project?

When sourcing natural stone from Iran, the three most common choices are travertine, marble, and granite. Each has distinct characteristics in appearance, durability, cost, and ideal applications. Understanding these differences helps architects, contractors, and importers specify the right stone for each project.

## Appearance and Aesthetics

Iranian travertine is known for its warm, earthy tones — beige, silver, red, and white — with a characteristic textured surface from natural voids. Marble offers a more refined, luxurious look with veining patterns; Pietra Gray is dramatic, while Dehbid white is elegant and subtle. Granite has a speckled, crystalline appearance in colors ranging from red and pink to gray and black.

## Durability and Weather Resistance

Granite is the hardest and most durable of the three, making it ideal for high-traffic flooring, exterior cladding, and monuments. Marble is moderately durable — suitable for interiors and sheltered exteriors but susceptible to acid etching. Travertine is softer and more porous, requiring sealing for exterior use, but performs well in warm, dry climates.

## Cost Comparison

Travertine is generally the most affordable, with FOB Bandar Abbas prices starting from $150/ton. Marble sits in the mid-to-premium range ($200–$500/ton), with Pietra Gray and white Dehbid at the upper end. Granite is mid-range ($150–$300/ton), with red granite from Natanz commanding a premium.

## Best Applications

- **Travertine**: Facades, exterior wall cladding, flooring in warm climates
- **Marble**: Interior flooring, wall panels, countertops, luxury features
- **Granite**: High-traffic flooring, kitchen countertops, monuments, exterior cladding
